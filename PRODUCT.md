# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People visiting Sayed Elshazly's portfolio to understand his professional background and explore his interests through blog articles.

## Product Purpose

Present Sayed's backend engineering work alongside his automotive quality-inspection role and interests in content creation and cars. Success means visitors can quickly understand this combination and find articles they want to read.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines a backend engineer's professional profile with a personal editorial space for automotive interests and content creation.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through the portfolio, use its navigation and theme control, and browse profile, project, and blog content on desktop or mobile web.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing site is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript portfolio with dark and light themes.
- The requested blog is a separate page linked from the existing header and should reuse the site's navigation and theme preference.
- The user requested a few initial article drafts about content creation and the automotive world.
- No employer, published article archive, personal anecdotes, detailed inspection procedures, or external profile URLs have been supplied. Do not invent them or present drafts as previously published work.

## Brand Commitments

- The site is for Sayed Elshazly.
- Preserve the existing dark/light theme behavior and charcoal, warm-white, and red identity.
- Keep the portfolio's direct, professional voice while allowing the blog to feel personal and editorial.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing homepage describes Sayed as a backend engineer focused on PHP and Laravel.
- The user states that he works as a quality inspector in the automotive field and is interested in content creation and cars.
- No authored article notes, car photographs, employer name, or article publication history were provided.

## Product Principles

- Make Sayed's different professional interests easy to understand.
- Keep personal claims grounded in details he has supplied.
- Make the blog comfortable to browse and read on mobile and desktop.
